About Us

Rooted In Rock.
Built For Nepal.

Osiris Engineering and Consulting Service Pvt. Ltd. is a group of geo-science professionals established to provide complete geo-engineering services and solutions for hydropower and infrastructure projects. Previously operating as Geo Concern Nepal Pvt. Ltd., we bring deep expertise in Engineering Geology.

From macro-level national infrastructure to micro-level community projects, we deliver feasibility studies, geological investigations and mapping, geophysical surveys, hydropower design, construction supervision, mining exploration, and environmental studies.

Founded by experienced Geologists and Engineers, our team combines advanced academic qualifications with extensive field experience across Nepal's diverse and challenging terrain.

Our Vision
Shaping resilient projects with the power of engineering geology — providing time-friendly and cost-saving solutions.
Our Mission
To ensure our customers receive the best and newest service with advanced resources in Engineering and Geological Solutions.
Our Goal
To lead the Engineering and Geological Solution sector of Nepal.
